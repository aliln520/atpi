About Us

Official Campus Merch Hub — Universitas Indonesia

Hello!

Getmerchui combines "Get," "Merchandise," and "UI," signifying the act of obtaining UI merchandise. It reflects our goal to help buyers easily discover and acquire the products they want.

We are a digital promotion service for Universitas Indonesia's academic community, helping organizations and committees promote their merchandise and webinar tickets to reach a wider campus audience.
